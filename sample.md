# Slidecraft
Beautiful slides from plain Markdown

---

## Why Markdown?

- Write in any editor
- Focus on **content**, not formatting
- Version control friendly
- Easy to convert into *anything*

Note: Speaker notes go after a line that starts with "Note:". They are not shown on the slide.

---

## How it works

1. Write or upload your Markdown
2. Preview your slides in real time
3. Pick a theme
4. Enhance the text with AI
5. Export to PDF
